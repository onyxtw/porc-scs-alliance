# G.E.M.S. & T.C.P. Overview

## G.E.M.S. (Geophysical Element Metabolic System)

Models subsurface and geophysical metabolic processes that interact with
compute waste heat and energy flows.

## T.C.P. (Terra‑Compute Protocol)

Defines how compute workloads and physical infrastructure are coordinated
to maintain resilience and negative-carbon operation at regional and global scales.
