# PoRC‑SCS Alliance Governance

## Councils

- Technical Council
- Energy & Geophysics Council
- Sovereign & Civil Council

## Responsibilities

- Maintain PoRC Index Core and JSON Schema.
- Oversee geophysical resilience and energy alignment.
- Ensure public-interest alignment and long-term resilience.

## Decision Process

- Proposals via Alliance repositories.
- Review by relevant councils.
- Ratification via documented governance procedures.
