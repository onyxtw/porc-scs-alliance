# PoRC‑SCS Tier Map

## Tier‑0

- Physical-origin, foundational reference nodes.
- Used for baseline calibration and long-term resilience studies.

## Tier‑1

- Regional nodes with strong geophysical and energy integration.
- Bridge between Tier‑0 and global deployments.

## Tier‑2

- Broad global deployments across hyperscalers and sovereign infrastructures.
